About Us

JNEC Online Fitness Gym is your digital solution for efficient gym management, allowing users to book gym, track schedules, can view equipment details and receive personalized notifications.

Designed in May 2025 as a student project, the system aims to automate gym operations and replace slow manual workflows, thereby enhancing the user experience and engagement within the college community.
