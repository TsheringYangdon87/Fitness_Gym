About JNEC Fitness System

JNEC Online Fitness Gym Management System is a comprehensive digital solution for efficient gym administration, enabling automated membership management, equipment tracking, scheduling, and member engagement.

Designed in May 2025 as a student project, the system streamlines gym operations and replaces manual workflows, enhancing administrative efficiency and user experience within the college community.
